❓ Question of the Day

Why is Private Video Recorder different from other recorders?

Sure, you might say, “Well, webcamera.io also says it's secure and private!”

There's an important difference, though. According to webcamera.io's own privacy policy , data provided while using its services is stored on its servers and automatically deleted no later than 12 hours after you finish using the service.

Private Video Recorder takes a different approach. Your recording stays in your browser. There is no video-upload system or recording backend in this project.

That means your recording doesn't need to be sent to a remote server just to be processed. Record it, preview it, and save it directly to your own device.

🔒 No upload. No server-side recording storage. Just your browser.
